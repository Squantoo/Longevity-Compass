What is a compounded GLP-1 program?

GLP-1 medications like semaglutide and tirzepatide are prescription treatments used in medically supervised weight management. Through telehealth platforms, a licensed provider reviews your health history and, if appropriate, can prescribe a compounded version — a formulation prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy rather than a mass-manufactured branded product.

This is an important distinction: compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The FDA does not review compounded drugs for safety, effectiveness, or quality before they're dispensed. Branded GLP-1 medications go through FDA approval; compounded versions do not. Whether a compounded option is appropriate for you is a decision only a licensed provider can make, and not everyone will qualify.

Both platforms below operate on the same basic model: a quick online health assessment, provider review, and — if prescribed — home delivery, with ongoing messaging for adjustments and refills.

Head to head

Price

Trimi has the lower published entry point — semaglutide from about $99/month versus Telos RX's tirzepatide from about $129/month (oral) or $139/month (injectable). If your provider steers you toward semaglutide and budget is the priority, Trimi's starting price is hard to beat. Both use flat pricing that doesn't increase as your dose is adjusted.

Formats

This is the clearest difference. Trimi offers a weekly injection. Telos RX offers both a weekly injection and a needle-free daily oral tirzepatide with built-in anti-nausea. If avoiding needles matters to you, Telos RX is the only one of the two with that option.

Shipping & support

Both include free shipping and 24/7 messaging. Trimi advertises free overnight shipping; Telos RX advertises free 48-hour shipping. Practically, both get medication to your door quickly after a prescription is issued.

Licensing & safety signals

Both connect you with U.S.-licensed providers and fill through licensed 503A compounding pharmacies. Trimi additionally holds LegitScript certification, a third-party standard some people look for when vetting telehealth platforms. As always, the meaningful safety decision — whether a given medication is right for you — is made by your provider, not the platform.

How to choose

  • Choose Trimi if the lowest entry price is your priority, you're comfortable with a weekly injection, and you'd value a LegitScript-certified platform.
  • Choose Telos RX if you specifically want tirzepatide, prefer a needle-free oral option, or want pay-over-time financing.
  • Either way, the medication and dose are decided by a licensed provider after reviewing your health history — so the practical first step is completing an assessment and seeing what you qualify for.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Both platforms require an online health assessment reviewed by a licensed provider, who decides whether a prescription is appropriate. Not everyone will qualify.
