Advertising Disclosure

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Longevity Compass is a free resource. To keep it free, we use affiliate (partner) links. This page explains what that means, in plain language, in keeping with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidance on disclosing material connections.

How we make money

When you click certain links on our site and then enroll or make a purchase with a telehealth platform, we may earn a commission or referral fee from that platform or its affiliate network. This never costs you anything extra — the price you pay is the same whether you use our link or go directly.

Which links are partner links

Many of the outbound links to the platforms we feature — including the "Visit" buttons on our comparison cards — are partner links. We mark paid links with the sponsored attribute, and a disclosure appears near the top of pages that contain them. If a link is not a partner link, we still only share it because we believe it's useful.

How compensation affects our content

We want to be straightforward about this: we generally can only earn from, and therefore tend to feature, platforms we have a partner relationship with. However:

  • We only list platforms that meet the standards described in our Editorial Policy — licensed providers and licensed pharmacies, no grey-market sellers.
  • The order in which we rank or recommend platforms is based on our editorial criteria — clinician access, pricing transparency, options, and support — not on which platform pays us the most.
  • If compensation ever influences placement, we will say so on that page.

Not medical or financial advice

Our content is for general information only and is not medical advice. Treatment decisions are made between you and a licensed provider. See our Terms of Use for more.
