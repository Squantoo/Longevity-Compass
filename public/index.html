About us

Our mission

We believe that access to safe, effective preventative care shouldn't depend on your zip code, your insurance plan, or your bank account. Longevity Compass was built to close that gap — connecting you with licensed telehealth platforms that work with verified pharmacies, so you can get the support you need without the runaround.

Why we started

Too many people spend years searching for answers, bouncing between providers, or giving up entirely because the system wasn't built with them in mind. We started Longevity Compass to change that — to make the path to better health simpler, more transparent, and more human.

Our approach

Every platform we partner with is vetted for quality, safety, and compliance, so you can feel confident in what you're getting and where it's coming from. We handle the vetting so you can focus on what matters: your health, your goals, and your timeline.

What sets us apart

Verified partners only

The telehealth platforms we list work exclusively with licensed 503A and 503B pharmacies, and we vet each platform before adding it to our network.

Accessibility first

We work to remove the cost and logistical barriers that keep people from preventative care.

You're in the driver's seat

We provide options and information; you make the decisions that are right for your body and your life.
