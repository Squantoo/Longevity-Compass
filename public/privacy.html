Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Longevity Compass ("we," "us") handles information when you visit our website. We keep data collection to a minimum.

Information we collect

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we (and our service providers) automatically collect basic technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and the site you came from. This is used to operate and improve the site and understand traffic.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for two main purposes: analytics (to measure how the site is used) and affiliate attribution. When you click a partner link, our affiliate partners and their networks may set cookies so that a referral can be correctly credited if you enroll. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

Information you provide

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We currently do not require accounts or collect payment information on this site.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website;
  • To measure traffic and content performance;
  • To attribute referrals to our partners for commissions;
  • To respond to messages you send us.

Third parties

We share information with service providers that help us run the site (such as analytics providers) and with affiliate networks and partner platforms for referral attribution. When you click a partner link, you leave our site and the partner's own privacy policy governs any information you provide to them. We do not control those third parties. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain sharing. To make a request, email hello@longevity-compass.com. You can also control cookies via your browser and opt out of many analytics/advertising cookies through your device settings.

Adults only

This site is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us about privacy at hello@longevity-compass.com.
