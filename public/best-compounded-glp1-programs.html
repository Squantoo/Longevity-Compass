What is a compounded GLP-1 program?

GLP-1 medications like semaglutide and tirzepatide are prescription treatments used in medically supervised weight management. Through a telehealth platform, a licensed provider reviews your health history and, if appropriate, can prescribe a compounded version — a formulation prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy rather than a mass-manufactured branded product.

The key distinction: compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The FDA does not review compounded drugs for safety, effectiveness, or quality before they're dispensed. Branded GLP-1 medications go through FDA approval; compounded versions do not. Whether a compounded option is right for you is a decision only a licensed provider can make, and not everyone will qualify.

Head to head

On entry price, Trimi leads — semaglutide from about $99/month, versus Telos RX from $129/month (oral tirzepatide) and Ageless from $199/month. Ageless is the priciest to start, but its GLP-1 plans bundle B12 and it covers more verticals; Trimi is the pick if the lowest number is the priority. Formats

Trimi offers a weekly injection. Telos RX adds a needle-free oral tirzepatide (with built-in anti-nausea). Ageless offers injections plus needle-free sublingual drops. If avoiding needles matters, Telos RX and Ageless both have options; Trimi is injection-only.

Breadth

This is where Ageless separates itself. Trimi and Telos RX are focused primarily on weight (Telos also offers some longevity options), while Ageless spans GLP-1 weight loss, TRT, NAD+, and sermorelin. If you expect to pursue more than one goal, consolidating with one provider can be simpler.

Licensing & safety signals

All three connect you with U.S.-licensed providers and fill through licensed pharmacies. Trimi additionally holds LegitScript certification, a third-party standard some people look for. As always, the decision that matters most — whether a given medication is right for you — is made by your provider, not the platform.
